Investment Overview

Hodges Ward Elliott ("HWE") is pleased to present the opportunity to acquire the 85-key Courtyard and 88-key TownePlace Suites Galveston Island (the "Hotels" or "Properties"), renovated in 2024 and ideally positioned along Galveston's premier waterfront. Located directly on the Gulf of America, the Properties benefit from convenient access to the island's renowned beaches, entertainment attractions, and corporate offerings, including proximity to Moody Gardens, the Port of Galveston, and the Galveston Island Historic Pleasure Pier. Galveston Island is a major tourism destination, attracting approximately 9.1 million annual visitors and benefiting from the Port of Galveston, the fourth-busiest cruise port in the United States, which generated approximately 3.6 million passenger movements in 2025. Supported by a diverse mix of leisure, cruise, and extended-stay demand, the Courtyard and TownePlace Suites are well-positioned to capitalize on Galveston's continued growth as a leading Gulf Coast destination, presenting an attractive opportunity to acquire a recently renovated, waterfront portfolio.

A Healthy Tourism Industry

Galveston Island represents one of the premier tourism destinations along the Texas Gulf Coast, supported by a substantial visitor economy, renowned coastal attractions, and a growing cruise industry. In 2025, Galveston welcomed approximately 9.1 million visitors who generated $1.3 billion in direct visitor spending and $1.7 billion in total economic impact. Tourism serves as a critical economic engine for the island, supporting approximately 12,700 jobs, representing nearly one-third of all employment, and generating $408 million in personal income. Lodging represents the largest category of visitor spending, accounting for approximately $392 million, or 30% of total visitor expenditures. The market also benefits from a diversified tourism base spanning leisure travelers, overnight visitors, and cruise passengers, with 3.6 million passenger movements in 2025. Additionally, visitor spending generates approximately $123 million in state and local tax revenue, underscoring the importance of tourism to Galveston's economic vitality. With its established reputation as a premier beach destination, significant cruise activity, and continued investment in tourism-related attractions and infrastructure, Galveston offers a compelling lodging market supported by substantial and diversified visitor demand.

Moody Gardens

Less than 10 minutes from the Hotels, Moody Gardens is one of Galveston Island's premier tourism and entertainment destinations, serving as a major year-round demand generator for the island's hospitality industry. Situated on more than 240 acres, the expansive complex features three iconic glass pyramids housing a 1.5-million-gallon aquarium, a tropical rainforest with more than 1,700 exotic plants and animals, and interactive science exhibits. Additional attractions include a 3D theater, seasonal waterpark featuring a 650-foot lazy river and two 18-foot-tall tower slides, ropes course, zipline, and golf course. The venue also features the Moody Gardens Spa and Convention Center, which offers approximately 100,000 square feet of meeting and event space, positioning the development as a significant draw for leisure travelers, corporate groups, conventions, and special events. Originally developed to extend Galveston's tourism season beyond the traditional summer months, Moody Gardens generates annual attraction revenues of more than $36 million, with additional indirect spending benefiting businesses throughout the island. Its diverse attractions, educational programming, seasonal events, and extensive event facilities generate consistent visitation throughout the year, making Moody Gardens one of Galveston's most significant tourism demand drivers and an important contributor to the island's lodging market.

Port of Galveston

The Port of Galveston operates as one of the most significant economic engines on the Texas Gulf Coast and has established itself as a premier cruise destination in North America. As the only cruise homeport in Texas and the fourth-busiest cruise port in the United States, the Port recorded approximately 3.6 million passenger movements across 416 sailings in 2025, generating an estimated $880 million in cruise-related economic impact. Cruise operations support over 4,500 jobs, generate roughly $300 million in personal income, and contribute more than $730 million in local business revenue. The Port continues to expand its prominence as a major U.S. cruise gateway, having opened its fourth cruise terminal in 2025 with a $156 million investment to accommodate the growing presence of MSC Cruises and Norwegian Cruise Line, joining Carnival, Disney, Princess, and Royal Caribbean. The facility now encompasses four cruise terminals totaling approximately 715,000 square feet, more than 8,800 port-operated parking spaces, and over 400 annual cruise sailings. Beyond its cruise operations, the Port serves as a significant commercial and cargo hub, handling approximately 2.9 million tons of cargo in 2025 and creating a total economic impact of $6.5 billion. With continued investment in cruise infrastructure, expanding passenger volumes, and an increasingly diversified lineup of cruise operators, the Port of Galveston serves as a substantial and growing lodging demand generator, supporting overnight stays from pre- and post-cruise passengers, cruise-related personnel, and associated business travelers.
